You are designing the title treatment and main menu for an indie video game called **The Legend of Maung Pauk Kyaing**. Use that exact English title, with "Maung Pauk Kyaing" spelled exactly like that.

## The game in five lines

- A 2.5D action-adventure puzzle platformer with a JRPG feel, based on a Burmese folk tale set in the ancient city of Tagaung, in Myanmar.
- Maung Pauk Kyaing is a young scholar who leaves the mountain academy of Taxila with no sword and no magic, only three maxims from his master: ask questions, stay awake, keep your mind sharp.
- In Tagaung every groom of the cursed princess is found dead by morning. He survives the wedding night by setting a banana-trunk decoy in his bed and hiding in the rafters, and a secret dragon king (a naga) bites the decoy and is slain.
- Then he must answer the princess's deadly riddle in front of the whole court, and he wins by listening, not fighting.
- He writes what he learns in a notebook. The theme is wit over weapons.

Mood: a warm, storybook folk tale with real dread at night. Think lantern light, mist, gold leaf, and a small clever hero in a big dangerous palace.

## The in-game look the design must match

The game uses an "HD-2D" look: hand-painted 2D pixel-style characters standing in a lit 3D diorama, with tilt-shift blur, soft depth of field, bloom on lanterns and warm light against cool blue shadows. The title should feel like it belongs in that world. Build it from materials the player sees in the game: black lacquer, gold leaf, brick and teak, palm-leaf manuscript pages, ink and brush. Do not use glossy chrome, plastic bevels or generic fantasy-RPG metal lettering.

## What to design

1. **Three title directions** to compare, each a full lockup of the title text:
   - **Lacquer and gold:** black lacquer letterforms with gold-leaf inlay, a naga (serpent-dragon) coiling through or around the letters.
   - **Kalaga tapestry:** raised, embroidered letters with sequin and thread texture on deep red or indigo cloth, in the style of Burmese kalaga tapestries.
   - **Palm-leaf manuscript:** incised and inked letters on a palm-leaf page, as if written in the hero's notebook, with the ink appearing to be written by hand.
2. **Pick the strongest direction** and refine it into a final logo system: a main lockup (two-line, centered), a horizontal lockup, and a small square mark or monogram for icons.
3. **A main menu screen mockup** at 1920x1080 (16:9) using the final logo, built to be a real game screen:
   - Scene: Tagaung at night. A tiered palace roof (a *pyatthat*) and gilded stupas against a moonlit sky, lantern-lit rooftops, drifting mist, banana-tree silhouettes in the foreground. The hero is a small figure with white student robes, a red scarf, a cloth satchel and a plain dagger, standing in the lower third. A naga shape appears subtly in the clouds, in the architecture, or in the shadows. It should feel a little ominous.
   - Layout: the logo in the upper-left or upper-center, taking about 45% of the screen width. Menu items on the left or lower-left, with room to breathe: New Game, Continue, Options, Credits, Quit. A small label reading "Act I Playtest Build" in a corner. A language switch placeholder that just says "English" and "Burmese".
   - Make it feel alive without animation: show light, mist and depth in layers that could be split into parallax layers for the game.
4. **Motion notes** (a short description, not a video): how the title could appear when the game starts. Suggested idea: the title writes itself in with an ink reveal, as if the hero were writing it in his notebook, then lantern light flickers across it.
5. **An itch.io cover image** at 630x500, using the same art, with the title readable at small thumbnail size.

## Typography

- The title is two levels: "The Legend of" smaller, and "Maung Pauk Kyaing" large and dominant.
- Letterforms should look hand-crafted. Custom-drawn lettering is best. If you use an existing font, use only fonts under a commercial-use licence such as the SIL Open Font License, and list each font and its licence at the end.
- Do **not** invent or fake Burmese script. Leave a clear, intentional space in the lockup for a Burmese-script title line that a Burmese designer will add later.

## Cultural care (important)

The game is made with respect for Myanmar and will be reviewed by Burmese advisors. Draw only from Burmese sources: kalaga tapestry, lacquerware, Bagan-era temple murals, gilded stupas, tiered palace roofs, palm-leaf and folding-book (*parabaik*) manuscripts, and the naga. Avoid:

- Elements from other cultures presented as Burmese, such as Thai, Khmer, Indian or generic "East Asian" brush lettering, pagodas or dragons.
- Buddha images, monks or sacred symbols used as decoration or in a violent, comic or commercial way.
- Stereotypes, "exotic" fonts, or anything that looks like a tourist souvenir.
- Real people or real logos.

## Colour

Suggested starting palette (adjust as the design needs): deep indigo night, black lacquer, gold leaf, vermilion red, jade green, and warm lantern amber. Keep the hero's red scarf as a small bright accent. Make sure the title is readable on dark and light backgrounds, and provide a one-colour (monochrome) version.

## Output

- Vector logo files (SVG), with the text, ornaments and background on separate layers.
- Transparent PNG exports of the logo at 4K width.
- The main menu mockup and the itch.io cover as images.
- A short guide: palette with hex codes, fonts and licences, minimum logo size, spacing rules and a few "do not" examples.
- A short note on why you chose the final direction.

Quality bar: the main menu should look like it belongs in a polished indie game on first sight, and the title should be recognisable even as a tiny icon.
